## Ingredients

### Noodles
- 8 oz spaghetti
- 1 tbsp salt, for the pasta water

### Sauce
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- Black pepper, to taste

## Steps

1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until just al dente. **Save 1 cup of pasta water** before draining.
2. Melt the butter in the same pot over medium-low heat and cook the garlic for 1 minute, until fragrant.
3. Add the noodles, parmesan and a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy.

   ![Glossy noodles in the pot](garlic-noodles-step-3.jpg)

4. Season with lots of black pepper and serve right away.

## Notes

- No parmesan? Any hard cheese works.
- Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge.
